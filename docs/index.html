WidgetApp

An Android app with five home screen widgets: quotes, your own photos, generated art, mazes and a game of Plinko. No account, no ads, and nothing leaves your phone.

Download APK All releases

The newest version. Needs Android 8.0 or newer.

The widgets

Believe you can and you’re halfway there.

Quote or fun fact

Shows a new quote or fun fact every so often, on one of eight photos. You choose which kinds you want to see.

Image

Shows your own photos, one after another. You add them in the app, and they stay on your phone.

Generated images

Moving art made from noise. You pick the colours, the kind of noise (value, Perlin or cells), how detailed it is and how fast it moves. Every image is new.

This one is drawn by your browser the same way the app does it. Tap it for another one.

Maze 3×3

A maze with exactly one way from the green dot to the red one. Solve draws the route, New maze makes another, and every 30 minutes it makes a new one by itself.

Plinko 4×3

The game at the top of this page. Tap to drop a ball, build chains and climb the levels.

  • Every level gives you 5 balls and a number of points to reach. The bar under the level shows how far you are.
  • A ball that lands on +3 gives 3 points and turns into 3 new balls, which drop from the top again. The −1 in the middle takes the ball away.
  • All balls that come from one drop are a chain. From 15 balls the points count double, then ×3, ×5 and ×10.
  • One pin glows. A ball that bounces off it gives you an extra ball to drop.
  • You have 3 hearts. Missing a level costs one, and with none left you start again at level 1. The star is the highest level you ever reached.

Install

WidgetApp isn’t in the Play Store, so you install the APK yourself.

  1. Download the APK on your phone.
  2. Open it. Android asks if your browser may install apps; allow it.
  3. Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets and find Widget App.

After that, the app tells you when there’s a new version: tap Update in the bar at the top and your settings and widgets are kept. To move to a new phone, use Export your data in the settings. It only goes online to look for updates.
