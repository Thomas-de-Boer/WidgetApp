WidgetApp

Five widgets for your Android home screen: quotes, your own photos, generated art, mazes and a Plinko game. Everything runs on your phone. There is no account and no server.

Five widgets

Add as many as you like. Each one has its own settings in the app.

  • Quote or fun fact

    Shows a new quote or fun fact every so often. You choose which kinds you want to see.

  • Image

    Shows your own photos, one after another. You add them in the app, and they stay on your phone.

  • Generated images

    Shows animated art made from noise: value, Perlin and cell noise. Every image is new, and you choose how it looks.

  • Maze

    3×3

    Makes a maze with exactly one way out. Tap Solve to watch the path being drawn, or New maze for another one. It also makes a new maze by itself every 30 minutes.

  • Plinko

    4×3

    A game on your home screen. Tap to drop a ball through the pins, build chains and climb the levels.

    How to play
    • Dropping balls. Tap anywhere on the widget. Every level gives you 5 balls and a number of points to reach.
    • Slots. The slots give +4 +3 +2 +1 −1 +1 +2 +3 +4. The middle slot removes the ball. The other slots give that many points, and the ball turns into that many new balls, which drop from the top again.
    • Chains. All balls that come from one of your drops are a chain. A long chain multiplies the points: ×2 from 15 balls, then ×3, ×5 and ×10.
    • Bonus pin. Every level, one pin glows. When a ball bounces off it, you get an extra ball to drop.
    • Lives. You have 3. A failed level costs one, and when they’re gone you start again at level 1. The star shows the highest level you ever reached.

Install

WidgetApp isn’t in the Play Store. You install the APK yourself. You need Android 8.0 or newer.

  1. Download the APK on your phone.

  2. Open it, and allow installing apps from your browser or file manager when Android asks.

  3. Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, find Widget App, then drag a widget onto your home screen.

Updates

When you open the app, it checks GitHub for a new version. If there is one, a bar appears at the top. Tap Update to download and install it. Your settings, photos and widgets are kept.

Backup

In the settings you can export all your settings and photos to one file, and import that file again later, for example on a new phone.

Privacy

There is no account, and your photos and settings never leave your phone. The app only goes online to check GitHub for a new version, and to download it when you tap Update.
